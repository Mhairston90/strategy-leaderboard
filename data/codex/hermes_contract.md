# Paper-only Hermes operating contract

Hermes may read local paper-trading, research, and leaderboard artifacts.

Hermes may append review artifacts that propose one-variable hypotheses.

No real orders may be placed.

No credentials for real brokerage accounts may be used.

Hermes may not move money.

Hermes may not mutate trade logs, portfolio state, optimized strategy configs, foundry variant banks, leaderboard registry rows, exported CODEX snapshots, or automation schedules.

Human approval is required before any proposal changes strategy execution, allocation, routing, or leaderboard registration.
